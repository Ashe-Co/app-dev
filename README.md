# My Current Favorite Series: Detective Conan
 
## About
Shinichi Kudo is a brilliant high school detective known for solving complex cases. During an investigation, he is attacked by members of the Black Organization and forced to ingest the experimental poison APTX 4869, which shrinks him into a child while preserving his intelligence. To hide his identity and continue investigating the Organization, he adopts the name Conan Edogawa, inspired by mystery writers Arthur Conan Doyle and Ranpo Edogawa

### Why I like it
- Intense
- Bloody
- Thought provoking episodes
 
“If you let your emotions take over, you’ll never be able to see the truth.” — Edogawa Conan
